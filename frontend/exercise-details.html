Lower body · Foundational strength

Bodyweight squat

A simple, everyday movement to build strength in your legs and confidence in your body.

Move with confidence

Let’s take it step by step.

Slow and steady is a great place to start. Quality matters more than how many you do.

  1. 1

    Find your stance

    Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly. Let your arms reach forward for balance.

  2. 2

    Settle into your breath

    Gently brace your middle and keep your chest lifted. Take a comfortable breath in before you lower.

  3. 3

    Sit back and down

    Send your hips back as you bend your knees. Keep knees tracking in the same direction as your toes and heels grounded.

  4. 4

    Move within your range

    Lower as far as feels comfortable while keeping a neutral spine. Pause briefly without holding your breath.

  5. 5

    Press up with control

    Exhale and press evenly through your feet to stand tall. Keep a soft bend in your knees at the top, then repeat.

Take 3 seconds to lower, pause, then stand with control. Begin with fewer reps if that feels better.

Be kind to your body.

  • ✓ Keep knees following your toes — avoid letting them collapse inward.
  • ✓ Try squatting toward a chair for support and a comfortable depth.
  • ✓ Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort.

This guidance is educational, not medical advice. Ask a qualified professional if you’re unsure what’s right for you.
